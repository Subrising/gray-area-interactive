You've got a laptop, a kettle, a cat called Pip, and a loan from Mum.

Ship It: AI Dev Tycoon is a cozy management game about building software with AI. You take a brief, plan the build, choose a model, and spend a budget of tokens and hours. Then you ship it and find out whether the reviewers call it a Flop, Okay, a Hit, a Front page or Viral.

Plan, spend, ship

Fast or Careful. Honest or Bold. The frontier model or the cheap one. Tokens run out and deadlines are real.

A five-year career

20 quarters, 80 releases. Start at the kitchen table. Take the steady job, or go it alone and found a studio.

No hidden tricks

The game tells you what is likely, and that a sure thing can still fail.
