Description

You have a laptop, a kettle, a cat called Pip, and a loan from Mum.

Ship It: AI Dev Tycoon is a cozy management game about building software with AI. You take a brief, plan the build, choose a model, and spend a budget of tokens and hours. Then you ship it and find out if the reviewers call it a Flop, Okay, a Hit, a Front page or Viral.

Start at the kitchen table. Take the steady job at Megacorp, or go it alone and found a studio. Five years, 80 releases, one cat.

History

Gray Area Interactive started work on Ship It in September 2026. The idea was simple: a small, friendly game about how software gets made today, with all its trade-offs, deadlines and surprise outages.

The Ship It page and its launch waitlist opened in late October 2026. Short teasers come out each week on Instagram and YouTube. Steam is the main store at launch, and the mobile versions follow.

Features

  • Plan, spend, ship. Every job is a set of real trade-offs: Fast or Careful, Honest or Bold marketing, the frontier model or the cheap one. Tokens run out and deadlines are real.
  • A five-year career. 20 quarters and 80 releases. New model generations launch every year. They are smarter, louder, and sometimes down.
  • People who remember you. Mrs Hale at the corner shop, Ruth at Kettle & Co, and Dex at Loop Records trust you more when you keep your promises. Pay Mum back. She is not counting. She is a bit counting.
  • A rival who is sometimes right. Ace ships fast and bold. Some quarters it works.
  • The Herald, the deck, and the nights out. The local paper prints your launches and your flops. An event deck deals free-credit weeks, outages, and the odd AI that asks for a raise.
  • Fair by design. Every number the game uses is shown: the reviewers' luck ranges, the deck's odds and the tier marks.
  • A daily run. One seeded job a day, the same for everyone.
  • No ads. No accounts. No data collected. Plays offline.
