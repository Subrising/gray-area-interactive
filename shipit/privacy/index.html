Privacy Policy: Ship It: AI Dev Tycoon

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The game collects nothing. The waitlist keeps only your email address.

The game

Ship It: AI Dev Tycoon does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game has:

  • no accounts;
  • no analytics;
  • no advertising;
  • no tracking;
  • no crash reporting that leaves your device.

Your saves stay on your device. Your career and settings are stored in local files on your device. We never receive them.

Platform services. If you play through a store or platform, that platform may provide features such as cloud saves, achievements or leaderboards, for example:

  • Steam (Steam Cloud);
  • Apple (iCloud, Game Center);
  • Google (Play Games).

Those features are run by the platform under its own privacy policy, not by us, and you control them in the platform's settings. We don't receive any personal data from them.

The waitlist and launch email

What we collect. Only your email address, and only if you enter it in the waitlist form on this site. We collect nothing else. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Why. To send you one email when Ship It launches, and the odd launch news. When you join, we send you a short welcome email. You join the list as soon as you submit the form.

Who processes it. Kit (kit.com) stores the list and sends the email for us. Kit has its own privacy policy: https://kit.com/privacy. We do not sell or share your email with anyone else.

How to leave. Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us on the support page to delete your email.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children. The waitlist is for people who can give their own email address. Do not enter an address for a child under 13.

Changes. If this changes, we'll update this page and the date above before the change ships.
