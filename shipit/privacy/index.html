Privacy Policy: Ship It: AI Dev Tycoon

Last updated: 24 October 2026

We collect nothing. Ship It: AI Dev Tycoon does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no crash reporting that leaves your device.

Your saves stay on your device. Your career and settings are stored in local files on your device. We never receive them.

Platform services. If you play through a store or platform, that platform may provide features such as cloud saves, achievements or leaderboards (for example Apple iCloud or Game Center). The platform runs those features under its own privacy policy, and you control them in the platform's settings. We do not receive any personal data from them.

Children. Because we collect no data, we collect no data from children.

Changes. If this changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change ships.

Contact: open a request on GitHub.
